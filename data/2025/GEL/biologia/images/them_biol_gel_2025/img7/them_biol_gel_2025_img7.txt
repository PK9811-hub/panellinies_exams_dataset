Along the left side, labeled “Πρώτο γράμμα” (First letter), the rows are grouped by the first base (U, C, A, G). Across the top, labeled “Δεύτερο γράμμα” (Second letter), the columns are divided by the second base (U, C, A, G). On the right side, labeled “Τρίτο γράμμα” (Third letter), the possible third bases (U, C, A, G) are indicated vertically. Each cell of the table lists the four possible codons formed by combining the first and second bases with the four options for the third base, and next to each group of codons the corresponding amino acid is written in Greek along with its three-letter abbreviation in parentheses. For example, UUU and UUC code for Φαινυλαλανίνη (phe), while UUA and UUG code for Λευκίνη (leu). UCU, UCC, UCA, and UCG code for Σερίνη (ser). The codons UAU and UAC code for Τυροσίνη (tyr), whereas UAA and UAG are labeled as stop codons (λήξη). UGU and UGC code for κυστεΐνη (cys), UGA is another stop codon, and UGG codes for Τρυπτοφάνη (trp). Similar groupings are shown for codons beginning with C, A, and G, mapping to amino acids such as Προλίνη (pro), Ιστιδίνη (his), Γλουταμίνη (gln), Αργινίνη (arg), Ισολευκίνη (ile), Μεθειονίνη (met, also marked as the start codon “έναρξη”), Θρεονίνη (thr), Ασπαραγίνη (asn), Λυσίνη (lys), Βαλίνη (val), Αλανίνη (ala), Ασπαρτικό οξύ (asp), Γλουταμινικό οξύ (glu), and Γλυκίνη (gly). Overall, the diagram presents the standard RNA codon table, systematically showing how every possible triplet combination of U, C, A, and G corresponds to a specific amino acid or a translation stop signal.
