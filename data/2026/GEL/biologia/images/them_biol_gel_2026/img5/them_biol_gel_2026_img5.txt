Description
Figure 5: A circular map of a recombinant plasmid ("Πλασμίδιο") is displayed, showing various functional genetic regions and restriction enzyme cleavage sites. At the bottom, a section is labeled as the origin of replication ("Θέση έναρξης αντιγραφής"). Moving clockwise around the plasmid, there is a region labeled "Y", followed immediately by a restriction site for enzyme I ("ΠΕ_I"), which marks the beginning of the "Γονίδιο gfp" (GFP gene). The GFP gene region is shaded with diagonal lines and ends at a restriction site for enzyme II ("ΠΕ_II"). Further along the circle, another region labeled "Y" is present, followed by a region shaded with diagonal lines representing the ampicillin resistance gene ("Γονίδιο ανθεκτικότητας στην αμπικιλίνη"). Inside this resistance gene, a restriction site for enzyme III ("ΠΕ_III") is indicated.
