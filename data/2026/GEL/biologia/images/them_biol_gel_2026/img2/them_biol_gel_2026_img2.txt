Figure 2: A pedigree chart showing inheritance of a trait across three generations. Squares represent males, circles represent females. Filled symbols indicate affected individuals, while unfilled symbols indicate unaffected individuals. Generation I I-1: unaffected male. I-2: affected female. They are connected as a couple. Generation II (their children) II-1: affected male. II-2: unaffected female. II-4: unaffected male. II-2 is married to II-3, an unaffected male. Generation III III-1: affected female, daughter of II-2 and II-3. The pedigree shows affected individuals in every generation and includes an affected child born to two unaffected parents.
