Description
Figure 6: The recognition sequences and cleavage sites for three different restriction endonucleases (restriction enzymes), labeled as "ΠΕ_I", "ΠΕ_II", and "ΠΕ_III", are displayed. Each enzyme recognizes a specific palindromic double-stranded DNA sequence, shown with 5' to 3' polarity for the top strand and 3' to 5' polarity for the complementary bottom strand. Vertical arrows indicate the precise phosphodiester bonds cleaved by each enzyme on both strands. "ΠΕ_I" recognizes a 6-base pair sequence and cuts unevenly to leave single-stranded cohesive ends. "ΠΕ_II" recognizes a 6-base pair sequence and cuts near the edges, also leaving cohesive ends. "ΠΕ_III" recognizes a 4-base pair sequence and cleaves down the middle between specific nucleotides.
