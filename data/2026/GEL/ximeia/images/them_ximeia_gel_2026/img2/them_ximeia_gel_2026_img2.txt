The vertical y-axis represents the pH value, with specific gridlines marked at pH 5 and pH 7, as well as an upper dashed line representing the equivalence point pH. The horizontal x-axis represents the volume of the standard solution ("όγκος πρότυπου διαλύματος (mL)"), starting from 0, with two specific points marked: the equivalence volume ("V_ισ.") and the half-equivalence volume ("V_ισ./2"). The titration curve starts at an acidic pH below 5, rises smoothly to a plateau near pH 5 at the half-equivalence point, and then exhibits a sharp inflection vertical rise through pH 7 up to the equivalence point, before leveling off at a basic pH.
