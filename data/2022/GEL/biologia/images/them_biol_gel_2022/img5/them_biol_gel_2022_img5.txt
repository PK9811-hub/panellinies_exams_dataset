Figure 5: A pedigree diagram representing the inheritance of a trait across three generations. Individuals are represented by squares (males) and circles (females). Filled symbols indicate individuals affected by the trait, while unfilled symbols indicate unaffected individuals. In generation I, two couples are shown: individuals I-1 and I-2, and individuals I-3 and I-4. In generation II, the first couple has two sons labeled II-1 and II-2, while the second couple has two daughters labeled II-3 and II-4, with II-4 affected by the trait. Individuals II-2 and II-3 form a couple and have one child in generation III, labeled III-1, who is affected.
