Description
Figure 3: A reaction scheme showing a series of organic chemical transformations between compounds labeled with circled Greek letters. The scheme begins with an alkyne (αλκίνιο) labeled E, which reacts with H₂O in the presence of HgSO₄/Hg/H₂SO₄ to form compound A. Compound A reacts with compound B to produce an intermediate product (ενδιάμεσο προϊόν), which after reaction with H₂O and removal of Mg(OH)X forms compound Γ (C₅H₁₂O). Compound Γ can be oxidized with KMnO₄/H₂SO₄ to give compound Δ. Another pathway shows compound H, obtained as the major product (κύριο προϊόν) from the reaction of compound K with HCl. Compound H reacts with Mg in anhydrous ether (άνυδρος αιθέρας) to form compound B. From compound E, reaction with HCN produces compound Z, which in the presence of a polymerization catalyst (καταλύτης πολυμερισμού) forms compound I. Hydrogenation of Z with 3H₂ produces compound Θ.
