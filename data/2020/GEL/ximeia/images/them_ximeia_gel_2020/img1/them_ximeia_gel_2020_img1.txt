Figure 1: A set of four energy level diagrams labeled (α), (β), (γ), and (δ), showing transitions between discrete energy states. The vertical axis represents energy E, with a reference level marked as E = 0 at the top. Each diagram includes horizontal lines labeled E₁, E₂, E₃, and E₄, corresponding to different energy levels. In diagram (α), a downward arrow indicates a transition from E₃ to E₂. In (β), an upward arrow shows a transition from E₂ to E₃. In (γ), closely spaced levels are shown near E₁, with a downward transition between E₃ and E₂. In (δ), higher energy levels near E = 0 are shown with a downward transition from E₃ to E₂. The diagrams illustrate different possible transitions between quantized energy states.
