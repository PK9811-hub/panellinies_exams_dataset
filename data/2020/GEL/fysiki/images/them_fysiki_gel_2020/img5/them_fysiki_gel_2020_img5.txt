A diagram illustrating a complex mechanics problem. A uniform rod AΓ is hinged at A on a horizontal plane and leans against a smooth vertical wall at point Γ, making an angle θ=45° with the horizontal. Its midpoint is O. The inner cylinder has radius r, and the outer has radius R. Another string (2) wraps around the outer cylinder and hangs vertically, holding a mass Σ2 (m2). Directly below Σ2, separated by a vertical distance h, is another mass Σ1 (m1). Mass Σ1 rests on an inclined plane with angle φ=30°. Σ1 is attached to a spring with constant k, which is anchored at the top of the incline at Z. An axis pointing downwards along the incline is labeled (+).
