Description
A schematic of a Bainbridge mass spectrometer. Ions enter from a source through slits S1 and S2 into a velocity selector region. This region contains a downward-pointing electric field E (between a positive top plate and a negative bottom plate) and a magnetic field B1 directed into the page (indicated by 'X' symbols). An ion with charge +q is shown moving horizontally with velocity v, acted upon by an upward magnetic force and a downward electric force. Ions that pass straight through enter a second region with a magnetic field B2 directed out of the page (indicated by dots). In this second region, two isotopes with masses m1 and m2 follow semi-circular paths with radii R1 and R2, eventually hitting a photographic plate. The distance between the two impact points on the plate is labeled 'd'.
