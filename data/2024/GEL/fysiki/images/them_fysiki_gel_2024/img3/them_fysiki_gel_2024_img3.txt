Description
A schematic diagram illustrating the photoelectric effect and the subsequent motion of electrons in a magnetic field. On the left, wavy arrows labeled 'light rays' strike a rectangular hatched block labeled 'metal surface'. Electrons (e-) are ejected from the surface moving to the right. They enter a dashed rectangular region defined by vertices A, Γ, Δ, Z representing a 'High Vacuum Space'. Inside this region, there is a uniform magnetic field B indicated by circles with crosses (directed into the page). An electron velocity vector v is shown pointing to the right, perpendicular to the magnetic field lines.
